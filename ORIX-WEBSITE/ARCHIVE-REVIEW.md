Reviewed the root registry/protocol and PLAYGROUND material. Templates and capture/replay tooling were inventoried. Retained dependency bundles received targeted inspection.

Selected scenes received 21 additional dense extraction records. All 92 contact sheets were visually inspected, containing 1,656 decoded frames. Full recordings total 926.60 seconds, approximately 15 minutes 27 seconds. All extraction records have 36 frames and two existing sheets, with zero reported decoding errors.

This is temporal sampled review of the actual recordings. Continuous real-time playback, exact easing and perceived frame rate remain unmeasured. Source promotional videos, Rive/Lottie files, shaders and third-party assets were inventoried separately, with targeted source checks documented in the group reports. Consent overlays, blocked media and the archive's original checklist gaps remain part of the evidence limits.
