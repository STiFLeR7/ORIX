# ORIX website creative direction

The owner approved these images as inspiration on 2026-11-05. ORIX's existing identity, colors and product behavior govern their adaptation. The reference files are static images, so motion ideas in this document are design interpretations.

## Visual intent

Show the desktop layer as a tangible part of a Windows workspace. Use actual ORIX interface details as the principal product evidence. Focused lighting, layered compositions, controlled depth and tactile feedback can connect those details into a distinctive presentation.

The proposed dark presentation uses Obsidian and Cloud, with Slate detail and restrained Ice illumination. The supplied aperture and wordmark retain their geometry. Integration artwork retains the appropriate vendor shapes. Use Inter and the [visual-system guidance](TYPOGRAPHY-AND-VISUAL-SYSTEM.md) for supporting typography.

## Proposed composition priorities

1. Introduce ORIX through a desktop scene with its actual notch visible at a useful size.
2. Give visitors one clear demonstration in which a selected tool produces an understandable result.
3. Use readable dashboard close-ups to explain settings, integrations and appearance.
4. Select a restrained combination of supporting visual motifs during prototype review.

The arc and fan are alternative gallery compositions for comparison. Particle, code-depth and pixel treatments are optional accents. Final inclusion depends on coherence, accessibility, asset quality and performance.

## Product presentation

- Maintain the real interface's geometry, labels and state relationships when cropping screenshots.
- Show the collapsed notch, expanded layer and a tool-open state in their correct context.
- Keep key explanations and actions in selectable website text.
- Identify recreated interactions as an illustrative preview and retain a static product view.
- Use prepared sample notes, project names and account states for every public capture.
- Size sections around their content and useful product detail. Let the surrounding desktop establish scale.
